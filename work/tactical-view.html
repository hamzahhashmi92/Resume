Case 06 · AstraUTM platform

A tactical view for approving authorities

Authorities that approve flights close to take-off care about the next hour, not the whole day. I added a default view per authority and a tactical window that filters the dashboard.

  • React
  • Zustand
  • Floating UI

The problem

Every approver saw the full day of flight requests, even when only the next hour needed a decision.

My part

  • A default view (Today or Tactical) and a time window in each authority's settings.
  • A first-visit message that explains the view, and a floating switch on the dashboard.
  • Flight requests filtered to the tactical window.
  • A Zustand store with a typed view enum, shared by the switch and the filters.

Result

Approvers open the dashboard on the view their authority chose, focused on the flights that need them now.
