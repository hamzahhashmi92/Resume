Case 11 · SCNS, UAE

Organisation approval and payment requests

Commercial operators must be approved, and often pay a registration fee, before they can fly. I built the authority's review screens and one payment component used in four approval flows.

  • React
  • Reusable components
  • REST APIs

The problem

Users, organisations, drones and flight requests each needed the same "request a fee, or waive it" step during approval.

My part

  • The organisation review: trade licence, establishment card, owner's ID and documents, with approve, reject and activate.
  • A payment component driven by props, reused in all four approval flows.
  • Payment references, the rejected-applicant history and the system configuration page.

Result

One payment step behaves the same everywhere, and parts of these screens carried over into today's platform.

// one component, four approval flows
<PaymentInfobox
  fetchUrl={`/payments/organisation/${id}`}
  postUrl={"/payments/request"}
  paymentCategory={Payment.Organisation}
/>
