Case 10 · SCNS, UAE

The flight-request review page

Authority staff decide on every drone flight request from this page. I created it in 2022, extended it for four years, and in 2025 split it into 12 focused components.

  • React
  • Google Maps
  • REST APIs

The problem

The page had grown past 2,000 lines in one component, so every change risked breaking another section.

My part

  • The page itself: map area, pilot, drone, insurance, risk, billing, certificates, documents and comments.
  • 12 section components split out of the original file.
  • Amendments with coordinates in degrees-minutes-seconds or decimal, converted both ways.
  • Bulk download of all attachments, and a payment waiver for staff.

Result

Half of the page's code is mine: 2,800 of 5,600 lines, built over 247 commits.
