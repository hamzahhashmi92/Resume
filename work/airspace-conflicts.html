Case 08 · AstraUTM platform

Airspace conflicts while planning a flight

While a pilot draws a flight area, the form shows which zones it crosses and which approved flights it overlaps, before the request is sent.

  • React
  • Infinite queries
  • Mapbox GL

The problem

Pilots found out about restricted zones and conflicting flights only after the authority reviewed their request.

My part

  • An accordion of overlapped zones: limits, type, validity in UTC or local time, and terms.
  • A paginated list of overlapping flights on an infinite query, with loading, error and retry states.
  • The approver layer on the map, and a fix so overlaps update in real time.
  • 15 of the 17 test cases for these components.

Result

Pilots see conflicts while they plan, before the authority has to point them out.
